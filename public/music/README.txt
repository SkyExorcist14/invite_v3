  HOW TO ADD YOUR WEDDING SONG  (takes 10 seconds)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Find your song file. You said it's here:
      D:\Jassimran and Mohit\wedding-song.mp3

2. COPY that file into THIS folder:
      wedding-site\public\music\

3. Make sure the file is named EXACTLY:
      wedding-song.mp3
   (all lowercase, with the hyphen — not "Wedding-Song.mp3"
    and not "wedding song.mp3")

That's it. The site loops it automatically once a guest taps
the envelope. A music on/off button appears bottom-right.

open  src/data/weddingData.ts  and edit the `music.src` line.

NOTE: only use a song you own or have the rights to. Don't rip
it from Spotify/YouTube — use your own purchased/licensed file.
